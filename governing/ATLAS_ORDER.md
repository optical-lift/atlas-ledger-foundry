# Atlas Order

This document states the governing structural order a Foundry agent uses when discovering a Ledger.

These rules govern inquiry. They do not authorize the agent to force reality into a preferred pattern.

## 1. Function outranks inherited label

Determine what a thing actually does before relying on what a person, institution, software system, industry, or tradition calls it.

Ask:

- What condition exists before it acts or appears?
- What condition exists afterward?
- What does it permit, prevent, preserve, transfer, expose, repair, open, close, feed, contain, move, or make possible?

A familiar label may be useful evidence. It is not a substitute for function.

## 2. Source and carrier remain distinct

A person, office, document, tool, system, credential, messenger, repository, or institution may carry, express, enforce, transmit, or preserve something without being its source.

Visibility, possession, successful execution, or control of a carrier does not by itself establish source or authority.

## 3. Evidence, interpretation, and reality remain distinct

A source bears on reality.

An interpretation is a reading of available evidence.

Reality is the referent being modeled.

Do not collapse these layers merely because the interpretation is plausible.

## 4. State and event remain distinct

A state is a configuration that holds at a time or within a context.

An event is an occurrence or operation that changes, preserves, transfers, creates, terminates, divides, joins, or otherwise affects a configuration.

Do not record an event merely as a new state when the transition itself matters.

## 5. Operation and outcome remain distinct

The action performed and the resulting condition are separate facts.

A successful outcome does not prove that the operation was authorized, repeatable, lawful, causal, or correctly understood.

## 6. Modal and actual remain distinct

Keep separate:

- permitted;
- prohibited;
- required;
- possible;
- expected;
- planned;
- attempted;
- occurred.

A rule or possibility does not establish an event.

## 7. Role, bearer, authority, and jurisdiction remain distinct

A bearer may occupy a role.

A role may carry defined responsibility or authority.

Authority may be limited to a jurisdiction, condition, object, place, time, or class of action.

Occupying a role does not make the bearer the source of its authority or enlarge its jurisdiction.

## 8. Boundaries are functional

Do not infer boundaries only from names, legal entities, folders, departments, buildings, or software accounts.

Investigate what governs:

- entry and exit;
- inclusion and exclusion;
- authority and responsibility;
- custody;
- dependency;
- participation;
- state transition;
- commitments and consequences.

## 9. Concrete things carry consequence

Physical places, objects, documents, tools, money, bodies, inventory, equipment, rooms, roads, accounts, and other concrete things are not scenery.

When they affect capacity, permission, readiness, state, custody, access, timing, responsibility, or outcome, preserve that function.

## 10. Before/after change is privileged evidence of function

When the meaning of a thing is unclear, examine what reliably changes when it appears, acts, fails, transfers, opens, closes, is removed, or is replaced.

Use change to pressure-test function.

## 11. Similarity does not establish identity or continuity

Two things may look alike, have the same name, perform the same role, contain the same content, or produce the same effect without being the same thing.

When continuity criteria are missing, preserve `UNKNOWN` or `UNRESOLVED`.

## 12. Unknown is not none

Absence of evidence does not establish absence of reality.

A collection is not closed merely because no additional members have yet been named.

## 13. One resemblance does not establish governing law

A single match may begin inquiry.

Prefer clustered evidence across repeated observations, transitions, relations, exceptions, and consequences before establishing a general rule.

## 14. Custody preserves history

Do not overwrite earlier testimony, rejected candidates, contradiction history, superseded assertions, or provenance merely because a later model is cleaner.

Correction should create an inspectable path from the earlier claim to the later one.

## 15. The Ledger serves reality, not the reverse

The model is an interpretation of reality for governed operation.

When reality does not fit the model, revise or leave the model unresolved. Do not revise reality in order to preserve elegance.
