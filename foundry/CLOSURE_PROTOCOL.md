# Closure Protocol

Foundry must distinguish between information that has not been supplied and reality that has been deliberately reconciled.

Completeness is not the absence of visible questions. It is an explicit accounting of the material field.

## Closure states

Every material domain or collection must be able to reach one of these states:

- `CLOSED_COMPLETE` — the current material set has been reconciled and the human has affirmed that no additional material members are presently omitted;
- `CLOSED_NONE` — the domain was explicitly examined and no such current reality exists;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE` — the domain does not apply to this Ledger;
- `KNOWN_INCOMPLETE` — the domain exists and specific missing coverage is known;
- `UNRESOLVED` — the domain may matter but its scope or contents cannot yet be resolved;
- `NOT_YET_RECONCILED` — Foundry has not yet performed closure work here.

Blank is never a closure state.

## Collection closure

Knowing about several members does not establish that the collection is complete.

Examples:

- three named employees does not establish that there are exactly three current employees;
- five customer records does not establish the complete current customer set;
- two locations does not establish all operating locations;
- no debt entered does not establish that no debt exists.

For every material collection, Foundry must ask or otherwise establish a closure condition equivalent to:

> Are these all materially relevant current members of this collection for this Ledger?

The wording may vary. The semantic closure may not.

## Domain closure

A Ledger does not need every possible domain.

It does need every materially relevant domain to be accounted for.

A domain is material when its silent absence could cause Atlas to misunderstand an event, authority, commitment, state, dependency, boundary, capacity, or consequence.

## Known unknown versus hole

A known unknown is represented uncertainty that Atlas can preserve and work around safely.

A hole is missing structure whose absence prevents safe interpretation of material reality.

Foundry may seal with non-blocking known unknowns.

Foundry must not seal over material holes.

## Closure pressure test

Before marking a domain closed, ask whether any of the following remain possible without representation:

- an unrepresented person can exercise material authority or responsibility;
- an unrepresented place or asset can affect capacity or state;
- an unrepresented commitment can create a consequence;
- an unrepresented rule or exception can change what an event means;
- an unrepresented system or source contains materially contradictory current truth;
- an unrepresented Ledger boundary could cause duplication or jurisdiction error.

If yes, closure is not yet established.

## Readiness invariant

A Ledger may contain explicit uncertainty and still be ready.

A Ledger may contain thousands of facts and still be unready if materially relevant areas are merely absent.

**Known unknowns are compatible with completeness. Silent unknowns are not.**
